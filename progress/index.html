02Lines that never bend

  • !Never bend a documented price, dose or legal line to fit a layout. 249 zl open store, 179 zl/szt. in a personalized set. Doses exactly as documented; etykieta* where the document gives no milligrams.
  • !Absorption is od 40x do 100x, w zaleznosci od skladnika, plus the four per-ingredient figures. Never 43x.
  • !Organimo's hero carries a 4.8/5 star rating. We have no ratings, so that slot takes the documented specialist and shipping line instead. No invented rating, review, customer name, study or journal anywhere.
  • !Expert voices only, attributed by role, behind a visible makieta chip. Stage I sells nothing and every cart says so.
  • !Palette and typefaces stay ITP: navy, ivory, electric cyan, Inter, Space Mono. We take organimo's structure, rhythm, scale and devices, not its lavender.
